About the System

Transparent Infrastructure Monitoring

The Projects Progress Monitoring System provides a structured, role-based platform for the North Central Province Education Department to oversee school construction and repair projects from assignment through completion.

Each level of administration — from the Provincial Director to school-level staff — has a dedicated interface calibrated to their responsibilities, ensuring accountability and transparency at every tier.

  • 📌
    Province-wide Project Assignment The Provincial Director can assign construction and repair projects to any school across all zones and divisions.
  • 🏫
    Zone-scoped Administration Zonal Directors manage and monitor projects exclusively within their geographic zone, ensuring focused oversight.
  • 📈
    Progress Reporting with Evidence School-level users submit progress updates with percentage completion, descriptions, photographs, and PDF reports.
  • 📄
    Secure Document Storage Project reports are stored securely via Supabase and photos via Cloudinary, accessible only to authorised personnel.
  • 🔒
    Role-based Access Control Four distinct user roles — Provincial, Zonal, Divisional, and School — each with precisely scoped permissions.

System Users

Administrative Hierarchy

🏭
Provincial Director

Province-wide oversight. Assigns projects to any school. Views all statistics and progress reports across all zones.

🏠
Zonal Director

Manages all schools within their zone. Assigns projects to zone schools and monitors progress updates.

📋
Divisional Director

Views and monitors all projects within their division. Reviews progress updates and provides feedback comments.

🏫
School User

Submits progress updates for assigned projects including completion percentage, photos, and PDF reports.
